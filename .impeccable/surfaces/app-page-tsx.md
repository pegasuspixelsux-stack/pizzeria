# Surface brief — app/page.tsx (Pizzeria homepage)

Scope: single long homepage for Pizzeria, a Neapolitan pizzeria & bar in Punta del Este, Uruguay. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: diners and visitors to Punta del Este / La Barra choosing where to spend a day or evening. Job: understand what Pizzeria is, browse the three services, book a table. Proof/content: authored daylight coastal photography, three service experiences each with an accordion of specifics (sample menu / house notes / bar list), invented-but-plausible contact + hours. Constraints: no backend — reservation form is static UI only; nav links smooth-scroll within the page.

Memorable moment: the page is one bright coastal room built around a wood-fired oven, seen through the three services of a day — the midday, the dinner, the bar — not a grid of equal cards. Each service opens in place to reveal its specifics.

Unresolved: none. Brief pins structure (Il Tridente Positano layout) and, per the user's later instruction, a light airy palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One coastal room at Punta del Este across the three services of a day — midday, golden hour, late bar. Refuses the restaurant template of hero + equal menu cards + generic sticky booking bar; experiences are alternating editorial image movements with in-place accordions, not a card row.

OWN-WORLD: Airy light coastal — pristine white (#ffffff) containers, pale sea/sand section tints (#f7f9fc, #eff3f8), deep ink text (#0f172a / #1e293b), soft slate secondary (#64748b). Warm sand gold (#c29b38) as the fine accent (brand dot, datelines, chevrons, hairlines, focus); deep ocean blue (#0e5a76) for text links. Playfair Display display to 5.5rem tracking -0.022em; Plus Jakarta Sans body/UI. Full-bleed daylight photography under pale white daylight gradients — never dark vignettes. White glassmorphism nav (bg-canvas/80 backdrop-blur, hairline border), ink text, polished outline Reserve button. Thin gold dividers between movements.

STORY: The visitor sees a singular Neapolitan pizzeria and bar, feels the bright Mediterranean atmosphere through imagery and quiet motion, expands the services that interest them, and books a table via a styled static mockup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen daylight photograph of a warm wood-fired pizza, pale white gradient rising over the lower third. Fixed white-glass nav: "Pizzeria" wordmark + gold dot left; centered links The Pizza / Bar / Events; right an outline "Book a Table" pill. Hero copy anchored lower-left: gold tracked dateline "Punta del Este — Uruguay", Playfair headline in ink over three lines "A wood fire, / a dough made at dawn, / and the sea air.", one slate subtitle line, a ghost "See the pizza" link with a compass glyph. Faint scroll cue bottom-center.

FORM: Brief-pinned editorial restaurant homepage (Il Tridente Positano structure); #1 on the ordered list because the brief pins it. No concept-seed roll — the brief paints its own picture and names a site to mirror; the palette was then redirected light by explicit user instruction, which pins over the roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
